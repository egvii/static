### Article 39 (A to F)

A - All Livelihood Options: 
Sarkar koshish karegi ki male aur female sabko rozi-roti (livelihood) ke पर्याप्त aur barabar mauke mile


B - Bantwara (Distribution): 
Desh ke material resources ka bantwara is tarah se hona chahiye ki uska fayda puri society ko mile, na ki sirf kuch ameer logo ko.


C - Cash Concentration: 
Paisa aur wealth ek hi jagah concentrate nahi hona chahiye.


D - Dono ko Same Pay: 
Male ho ya female, agar kaam same hai toh dono ki salary bhi ekdam barabar honi chahiye.


E - Energy ka exploitation nhi hona chahiye): 


Mazdooron aur workers ki energy aur health ka exploitation nahi hona chahiye. Unse unki umar ya takat ke khilaf zabardasti kaam nahi karwana chahiye .


F - Future (Children): 

Bachon ke future ke liye unhe ek healthy environment dena hai aur unka childhood protect karna hai taki wo sahi tarah se grow kar sakein.


### Article 39A (Extra Trick)

*   39A - A for Advocate: Iska matlab hai Free Legal Aid (Muft kanooni sahayata) [1]. Iska maqsad ye hai ki garibi ki wajah se kisi bhi insaan ko insaf milne se roka na ja sake [1].
